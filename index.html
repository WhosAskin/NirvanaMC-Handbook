02

Travel, homes & teleports

Move safely, save important locations, and travel with friends.

Carry food, tools, and enough supplies for your first shelter. Stay still during random-teleport warm-up and set a home before building.
/spawn

Return to the survival spawn.

/warp <name>

Travel to a public warp.

/warps

Open the public warp list.

/rtp

Find a safe random survival location.

/back

Return to your previous saved location.

/sethome [name]

Save your current location. The default name is home.

/home [name]

Travel to one of your saved homes.

/homes

Open your clickable home list.

/delhome <name>

Delete a saved home.

/tpa <player>

Ask to teleport to another player.

/tpahere <player>

Ask another player to come to you.

/tpaccept [player]

Accept an incoming teleport request.

/tpdeny [player]

Deny an incoming request.

/tpcancel

Cancel your outgoing request.

/tptoggle

Enable or disable incoming requests.

Protect your work and share access without giving up ownership.

01Select

Choose the claim area.

02Inspect

Check owner and border.

03Trust

Add only chosen players.

04Party

Share with your group.

Party members automatically share build and interaction access to land owned by another party member.

Begin selecting the corners of a land claim.

/claim info

/claim list

List the land you own.

Display claim boundaries.

/claim untrust <player>

/party create <name>

/party invite <player>

/party invites

/party info

04

Money & the community market

Understand the two currencies at a glance.

NirvanaBucks

Your normal survival balance for players and merchants. Earn by gathering, selling, trading fairly, voting, and joining events.

NirvanaCoins

A separate whole-number balance for lasting unlocks and the permanent perk shop.

NirvanaBucks and NirvanaCoins do not convert into each other. Use /transactions to inspect your own currency history.
/balance

View your NirvanaBucks balance. Alias: /bal.

/pay <player> <amount>

Send NirvanaBucks to another player.

/baltop [page]

View the balance leaderboard.

/transactions [page]

Review your own currency history.

/coins

Open your NirvanaCoins account and permanent perk shop.

/buycoins <80|1000|9000>

Open secure checkout for a supported coin pack.

07

Chat, mail & personal style

Stay connected while keeping control of your inbox, channels, and screen.

Private chat

Message a player, reply quickly, or ignore when you need space.

Mail

Leave a message for a known player even while they are offline.

/msg <player> <message>

Send a private message. Aliases: /tell, /w.

/reply <message>

Reply to your latest private conversation. Alias: /r.

/ignore [player]

Toggle an ignored player or list your ignores.

/mail read

Read saved mail and mark it as read.

/mail send <player> <message>

Send mail to a known player.

/mail clear

Remove mail you have already read.

/channel <global|local>

Choose your public chat channel.

/afk

Toggle your away status.

Use /ignore for personal boundaries, /mail for offline friends, and /sidebar to choose what stays on screen.
08

Permanent command perks

NirvanaCoins unlock personal convenience commands. Ownership—not staff rank—enables them.

Movement

/fly and /speed change only your personal movement.

World view

/ptime and /pweather affect only what you see.

Portable tools

Carry crafting, storage, repair, and renaming tools.

Cosmetics

Wear a held item as a hat and keep your look personal.

/fly

Toggle purchased personal flight.

/ptime <time|reset>

Choose personal time: day, noon, night, midnight, dawn, or dusk.

/pweather <clear|rain|reset>

Choose personal weather.

/echest

Open your portable ender chest.

/workbench

Open a portable crafting table.

/anvil

Open a portable anvil.

/hat

Wear the held item as a cosmetic hat.

/trash

Open a disposable inventory.

/feed

Restore hunger.

/heal

Restore health and hunger.

/repair [hand|all]

Repair a held or equipped item.

/speed [walk|fly] <1-10|reset>

Set personal movement speed.

/rename <name|reset>

Rename the item in your hand.

Before buying a perk: preview the live details, confirm the price and balance, unlock it when ready, then toggle or reset it as needed. The shop displays the live price, donor discount, balance, affordability, and ownership before confirmation.
